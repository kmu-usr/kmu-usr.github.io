About Our Journey

The Sustainable Laonong Talent Cultivation and Health Development Plan is a long-term University Social Responsibility program that Kaohsiung Medical University conducts in Liugui and Taoyuan, along the Laonong River.

We translate the knowledge of a medical university into language that the community can easily understand, and bring it into the field: the Saturday STEAM Science Education Program, developmental assessments for young children, and community oral health education and health talks. Through this service, our university students learn communication and empathy, and see the place of their profession in society. Local children, through science classes and arts activities, discover their own possibilities.

We have walked this path together since 2015, and the project is now in its fourth phase. The figures below and the Project History record this journey.
